# Swarm Pepe: the site, from the swarm

Output: `artifacts/video.mp4` (MP4 video, left untracked for artifact upload).

The 30-second film speaks in the swarm's first person. It covers the single written brief, React and viem static build, browser contract reads, three tabs, and reveal mechanism. Graphics follow the grid, HUD, pixel typography, and hard cuts of [job launch-449](https://github.com/identity-md-launches/launch-449-make-25-second-video-swarm). They are explanatory film graphics; the video contains no recreated site frames.

The builder parses each renderer SVG into its original 24 × 24 rectangle grid. Every screen enlargement repeats source pixels by an integer factor. No portrait or trait combination is drawn by hand. The film displays no wallet address, ENS name, or personal data.

## Build and checks

With Python 3 and FFmpeg installed, `python3 source/build_video.py` rebuilds the MP4 offline from the checked-in SVGs and narration WAV. `python3 source/check_video.py` checks codecs, dimensions, duration, frame count, file size, front `moov`, and full decode. Both scripts also accept local `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` binaries in `test/scratch/` for a temporary build environment. The delivered MP4 is self-contained and does not need FFmpeg to play.

The final file was inspected at chapter frames and at 29.8 seconds. FFprobe confirmed H.264 video and AAC audio, 960 × 540 at 30 fps, 30.000 seconds. Audio is non-silent (mean −20.4 dB; peak −1.8 dB).

Limitations: the supply reading and portraits are a snapshot at the block above; the collection can change after that block. The film shows twelve verified tokens as examples while describing the site's full gallery. H.264's 4:2:0 color sampling can cause minor edge color bleed even though portrait scaling uses nearest-neighbour pixel replication.
